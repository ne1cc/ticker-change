Methodology

1. 12-1 cross-sectional momentum

Each ticker is ranked by its trailing 12-month return, excluding the most recent month (Jegadeesh & Titman, 1993). Excluding the last 21 trading days removes short-term reversal effects driven by liquidity and market-impact noise rather than the underlying medium-term trend.

2. Risk-adjusted ranking

The leaderboard sorts by the 12-1 return divided by trailing 1-year annualised volatility, so a name isn't ranked highly purely for being erratic.

3. Universe backtest

The universe chart simulates an equal-weight portfolio of the top 5 ranked names, rebalanced monthly, with a 7.5 bps round-trip cost charged on turnover at each rebalance.

4. Single-ticker backtest

The single-ticker view runs a time-series variant on that same ticker: long when its own 12-1 score is positive, flat (cash) otherwise, under the same cost assumption.
